# Migration to one-axis opening variants and project hook words

Existing EDLs, motion plans, captions and briefs are untouched. Two commands
behave differently.

## `motion variants`

Before, a variant replaced the first caption with any line and spread its
words evenly over the first cue's time, even when nobody said them, and
`--from-brief` added a generated question. A caption is a transcript, so
that put unsaid words in the speaker's mouth.

Now:

- a line the speaker says word for word may become the first caption, with
  its real word timings; any other line is on-screen text over the opening
  and the captions keep the transcript;
- each run varies one axis: `--axis text` (the opening line) or
  `--axis first_shot` (`--first-shot SOURCE:START-END`, or `--pickup` when
  the footage does not exist yet);
- the default is a 6 s preview; render the chosen variant with
  `--full --only NAME`. `deliver --hook-variants` needs that full render.

An `analysis/hook-variants.json` written before still loads, as a `text` set.
Its variants have no hypothesis; render them again to record one.

## `hook check`

The list of words that make an opening a concrete situation depended on one
niche. It is now empty in the package, and `hook check` judges a concrete
situation only when the project lists them. Put the project's own words in
`.social-video/hook-words.yaml` in the project root:

```yaml
situation:
  - kaw
  - mlek
  - poranek
```

Any category there (`situation`, `address`, `tension`, `promo`, `greeting`)
replaces the general one; the others stay. A `child:` list reads as
`situation:`.

## Briefs

A first line said off screen is detected by `zza kadru`, `poza kadrem` or
`off screen`, whoever says it, and is shown as text only unless the
recording carries it. The brief fields are now `voice_off_screen` and
`first_line_off_screen`; `context/brief.json` files with the old names load
unchanged.
